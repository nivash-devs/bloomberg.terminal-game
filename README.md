# Terminal Trader

A retro trading-terminal game. Start with **$1**, trade whole shares of 24 simulated stocks, from penny stocks under $1 to a $650,000 trophy stock, buy upgrades, and try to reach **$1,000,000** without going bankrupt.

**Play:** https://nivash-devs.github.io/bloomberg.terminal-game/

## How to play
- Pick a stock, choose how many whole shares, hit **BUY**. Sell when it's higher. Your net worth and cash are shown big at the top.
- Headlines on the newswire push prices up or down.
- Spend profits on upgrades: trend scanner, lower fees, more stocks, short selling, margin, a trading bot and more.
- Command bar: `BUY MOON 3`, `SELL NVDA ALL`, `SHORT TSLA 2`, `AAPL`, `HELP`.
- Keys: arrow up/down pick a stock, `B` buy, `S` sell, `/` focus the command bar.

Progress saves in your browser.

*All prices are simulated for fun. Not real market data. Not financial advice.*
